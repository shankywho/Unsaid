4. Rules to keep

Accent only for live states, the current question and the confirmed sentence. Primary buttons are light on dark.
Fragments are always muted mono with ellipses. Resolved sentences are always large sans with the faint glow.
Remove Instrument Serif and the amber, warm and beige tokens. Nothing serif anywhere.
One level of nesting. Timeline, facts and hypotheses are rows; the fact detail sits inline under its step.
Content is visible by default. Hero animation and shimmer run only under prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference, and the static frame is the confirmed state.
Use faint (#7A7A82) for readable tertiary text; ghost (#5A5A62) fails AA and is for hollow dots and disabled glyphs.
Eval, Results and the model and date line read from generated/eval.json. The numbers on these boards were copied from the 2026-10-04 08:26 UTC live run for layout only.
Settings keeps its current content and takes the new components; it is not redrawn here.
GitHub star count on the hero button is a slot; fetch at build time or hide when unavailable.
